## Working agreements
1. Team members respond to team chat messages within 12 hours on weekdays.
2. Every member attends scheduled meetings on time or informs the team at least 30 minutes beforehand if they cannot attend.
3. Every member need to contribute to the project.
4. If a member cannot finish a task by the deadline, they must inform the team at least 12hours beforehand.
5. Every member commits their work using a clear message that describes the change.

| Work items    | GitHub Issues    | Within 12 hours on weekdays                    |
| Files         | this repository  | Update files through commits and pull requests |
| Meetings      | Online platform  | Attend at a agreed time                        |

## Decision rules
- Product questions: The Product Owner makes the final decision after hearing input from all team members.
- Technical questions: The team discusses the options and aims for consensus within 24 hours. If consensus is not reached, a majority vote decides.
- Deadlock (no agreement after __ hours): If the team remains deadlocked after 24 hours, the team asks the teaching assistant for guidance at the next lab session.
- Where decisions are recorded: lab01/decisions.md
